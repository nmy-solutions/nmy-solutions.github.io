Who we are

Ivan Nemyrovskyi, individual entrepreneur registered in Georgia, trading as NMY Agency, decides how your data is used (the controller). Contact: ivan.nemyrovskyi@nmy.agency, WhatsApp +380 93 263 75 15 or Telegram @inemyrovsk.

What we collect and why

When you send the contact form we receive your name, phone number and optional message, plus the page address, the site or campaign link you came from, your device type and basic anti-spam signals (time spent on the form, number of key presses). We use them to answer your request and prepare an offer. Name and phone are required so we can reply.

Legal basis: steps you ask for before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, Art. 5(1)(j)). If you write on behalf of a company: our legitimate interest in answering business requests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who receives it

Formgong (individual entrepreneur Vitalii Nemyrovskyi, Ukraine) runs the form for us as our processor. It stores submissions in the EU and delivers them to us by Telegram. Its script counts form views, starts, submissions and drop-offs, without cookies and without storing IP addresses. To opt out, write to us or block scripts from formgong.com; the form still works.

GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts this site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

We work from Georgia. Neither Georgia nor Ukraine has an EU adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

Formgong deletes submissions after 30 days. We delete a request 12 months after our last contact, unless we start working together. Client records are kept as long as Georgian tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, delete or hand over your data, or to restrict or stop using it. Write to us using the contacts above; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the State Audit Office of Georgia, to the data protection authority in your EU country, or to the UK Information Commission.

We make no automated decisions about you.
