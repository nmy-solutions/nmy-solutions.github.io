06/ FAQ

Good to know before we start.

01What kind of projects do you take on?

Websites and web apps, Telegram bots and Mini Apps, crypto exchanges (DEX and CEX) and fintech payment systems, including SEPA. If your idea is close to one of these, we’re happy to talk.

02How does a project start?

Send us a message by email, WhatsApp or Telegram. We’ll have a short call about your goals, then come back with a plan, an estimate and the next steps.

03Do you work with clients outside Ukraine?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide and communicate in English.

04Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. We start by reviewing the code and architecture, tell you honestly what we find, and then agree on how to move forward.

05Do you support the product after launch?

Yes. We can monitor, fix and keep developing the product after release, so it keeps working as your business grows.
