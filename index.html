What happens on the free call?

You tell us what you need, in your own words. Ivan asks what it must do, by when, and what budget you have in mind. If we can help, you get a price for the Project Plan. If we can’t, we say so. Nothing to pay and nothing to sign.

Is it expensive?

You know the full price before the build starts. A Project Plan costs $500–1,500. A Build project starts from $3,000 in total, and the plan fee is part of it. The price is in the contract, and you pay in parts.

Why pay for a plan?

A price without a plan is a guess. In about one week you get what we will build in simple words, drawings of every screen, the exact price and date for the build, and what can go wrong. If you continue, we take this fee off the build price. If you stop, the plan is yours, and you can give it to another developer.

Others promise cheaper and faster

Maybe they can do it. Ask them to put the price and the date into the contract, and to show you a demo every week on a real link. Both are in our contract.

What if you disappear?

The domain and the accounts are in your name, and the code becomes yours as you pay for each part. So another developer can continue the work. You also see progress every week and you pay in parts, so you never risk the whole sum.

I am not technical. Is that a problem?

No. You tell us about your business in your own words. We write the plan in plain words and show you screens. Every week you open a link and see what is done.
