06 / FAQ

Good to know.

What projects do you take on?

Websites and web apps, Telegram bots and Mini Apps, crypto exchanges (DEX and CEX) and fintech payment systems, including SEPA.

How does a project start?

Fill in the contact form. After a short call about your goals we send a plan, an estimate and the next steps.

Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. We review the code and architecture first, tell you honestly what we find, then agree on the way forward.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes — monitoring, fixes and new features as your business grows.
