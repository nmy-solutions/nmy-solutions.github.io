CRM

A booking and CRM system built only for barbershops: online booking, Telegram reminders, client cards and payroll — one simple plan, no contracts.

IndustryBeauty / Services
ProductBooking & CRM SaaS
PlatformsWeb · Mobile web · Telegram
LanguagesEnglish · Turkish · Ukrainian

01 / Features

What it does.

24/7 online booking

A branded booking page where clients pick a barber, a service and a slot — any time of day.

Team calendar

Drag-and-drop calendar for every chair: reassign a booking in one move and schedule vacations.

Telegram & SMS reminders

Automatic reminders before each visit to cut no-shows.

Client cards

History, notes, preferences and photos — every client feels like a regular.

Payroll

Payroll for the team lives in the same system as bookings and clients.

No-show calculator

Shows owners how much missed bookings really cost the shop.
