CarrierScan

CarrierScan is a free AI detector: paste text or upload a file and see in seconds whether ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude wrote it, with a score for every section. No sign-up.

IndustryAI / EdTech
ProductAI text detector
Languages7, incl. English & Ukrainian
AccessFree, no sign-up

01 / Features

What it does.

Section-by-section scoring

Text is split into parts and each part gets its own likelihood score.

Paste or upload

Paste up to 2,000 words or upload TXT, MD, HTML, RTF, DOCX, ODT or PDF — or pick from Google Drive.

7 languages

English, Ukrainian, Kazakh, Uzbek, Estonian, Georgian and Lithuanian detectors.

Private by design

Files are read in the browser and the text is never stored.

Works from AI assistants

Can be used directly from your AI assistant.

Guides

Articles that explain how AI detection works and how to read the results.
